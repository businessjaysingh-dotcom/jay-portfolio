Why I’m reaching out to JKD

I saw JKD Agency focuses on scaling agencies through outbound. I built and worked on a client prospecting and appointment-setting system using Telegram, not email. The shared work is the outbound process: find a relevant signal, assess fit, start a conversation, follow through, and identify when a call is appropriate.

I personally closed $100K in brand acquisition at EVO Marketing. Separately, I have personally booked service and installation appointments through low-pressure outbound for a homeowner-service business. The campaign metrics below belong to a different, automated client project.

What I did with the conversations

I reviewed where the system’s qualification and follow-up rules failed, then changed those rules. For example, I corrected a filter that wrongly excluded smaller operators who could fit the entry-level offer.

The system also answered questions and shared relevant advice in one-to-one conversations. It respected timing and stopped pushing when someone was not ready. I tracked call offers separately from conversations so activity would not be mistaken for booked appointments or revenue.

Example from a real, anonymized follow-up: after a prospect said work was taking priority and they would return later, the system replied, “all good bro take your time, hit me when you're back.” It acknowledged the prospect’s timing instead of forcing an appointment. This was an automated Telegram reply, not an email script I personally sent.

How that transfers to JKD’s email work

The channel is different. I have not run a Clay-based cold-email campaign. The transferable part is the workflow discipline: clear qualification rules, specific context for the opener, appropriate follow-up, call triggers, and honest funnel tracking.

I’d want to learn your sourcing, verification, Clay, deliverability, and appointment-booking process, then apply those principles to a small, reviewed email task. I would not present the Telegram results as email results.
